First Visit Ritual Checklist

A clean phone-friendly guide for the first visit: what to bring, what to avoid, what to do, and what to say. This is not the formal move-in day and does not include private personal details.

Top Rules To Avoid

Read this first before anyone enters. These are the guardrails for the day.

Do Not Treat This As Move-In

  • Do not sleep over.
  • Do not nap in the house. A short seated rest is fine; lying down to sleep is too close to symbolic occupation.
  • Do not cook the first meal or do formal bed-setting.
  • Keep the day as first visit, cleansing, blessing, and setup only.

Who Should Not Lead Ritual Actions

  • Wife should not participate in Bai Si Jiao.
  • Wife should not roll pineapple, scatter salt/rice, mop, burn offerings, hammer, move furniture, or do physical ritual work.
  • If mother is Tiger, she should not be among the first entering and should not take part in opening ritual actions.

Kitchen North Wall

  • Wife should avoid the north side of the kitchen/stove area.
  • Do not roll pineapple there.
  • Do not bang, drill, paste, clean aggressively, or disturb that spot.

General Don'ts

  • Do not enter angry, quarrelling, or empty-handed.
  • Do not wear all-white or all-black.
  • Do not let contractors enter before the first-entry ritual is complete.
  • Do not sweep inward. Sweep from inner corners toward the main door.

Timing

Step-By-Step Process

Follow this sequence from top to bottom. Husband leads the physical ritual actions.

1

Pause Outside The Main Door

Arrive before the chosen time. Stand calmly outside with pineapple, fruit, rice, and salt ready. Do not rush in.

2

Knock And Announce

Knock on the main door 3 times. Speak clearly before opening.

Say: “We are the new owners of this home. We enter today with respect, good intentions, and a clean heart. May any old or unsuitable energy please leave peacefully. May this home welcome health, harmony, safety, and prosperity.”
3

Husband Enters First

Husband steps in first with the right foot, carrying rice or fruit. Wife follows calmly after. If mother is Tiger, she enters later only after the main ritual is complete.

Say: “Kai men da ji. Huat ah. May this home be peaceful, healthy, and prosperous.”
4

Open The House

Open windows and internal doors. Turn on working lights. Let air and brightness move through the home.

5

Roll The Pineapple

Start from the main door and roll the pineapple inward into the living area. Husband picks it up and continues room by room. Do not roll into toilets, bathrooms, or the kitchen north-wall/stove area. If there are multiple floors, do the main floor first, then carry the pineapple upstairs and roll gently into each usable room.

Do not throw hard or damage walls/floors. The point is symbolic movement of prosperity through the home.

“Huat ah!”
“Kai men da ji” - great luck on opening the door.
“Cai yuan guang jin” - wealth flows in.
“Shen ti jian kang” - good health.
“Chu ru ping an” - safe coming and going.

After the ritual, leave the pineapple in the home for a few days. Do not eat or throw it away immediately.

6

Scatter Salt And Rice

Husband scatters a small amount of sea salt and uncooked rice into the four corners of each room, including hidden corners. Skip toilets/bathrooms and avoid disturbing the kitchen north-wall/stove area.

Leave for at least one hour. Sweep outward later from inner corners toward the main door.

7

Pomelo-Leaf Cleansing

Use pomelo-leaf water to wipe/mop floors and mist corners. Move clockwise through the home. Wife does not do the physical cleansing.

After cleansing, wrap used pomelo leaves in red paper. Dispose according to your family practice; if burning, do it safely and only where allowed.

8

Inform The Earth Deity

Place fruits near the centre of the home or just inside the main door. Husband leads the announcement.

Say: “Tu Di Gong, Di Zhu Gong, and guardians of this land and home, we respectfully inform you that our family has received this home and will be caring for it from today. We ask for permission and blessing to enter, cleanse, renovate, and live here in peace. Please bless this household with safety, harmony, health, smooth renovation, good neighbours, and steady prosperity. If there are old or unsettled energies here, may they depart peacefully. Thank you for protecting this home.”

If you prefer Chinese phrases, add: “Jia zhai ping an, shen ti jian kang, cai yuan guang jin, chu ru ping an.”

9

Walk Through With Good Words

Walk the home together. Speak positive words only. Visualise family life, smooth renovation, safe movement in and out, and peaceful rest in the future.

10

Sweep Out And Close

Sweep salt/rice from inner corners toward the main door. Do not sweep inward. Thank the home and end the ritual calmly before vendors or contractors begin their work.

Full Traditional Bai Si Jiao

Use this if doing the fuller four-corners version. Wife should not participate in this ritual. Husband leads, or engage someone experienced.

Setup

  • Prepare 5 offering sets: 4 corners plus centre.
  • Each set can include fruit, rice, beans, peanuts/sweets, tea/wine, candles, incense, and ritual paper.
  • Place centre offering first, then the four corners.
  • Move clockwise.

Safety

  • Check if burning is allowed.
  • Use a fire-safe container.
  • Do not burn near curtains, paper clutter, or dry leaves.
  • Never leave candles or embers unattended.
A

Centre First

Light incense/candles at the centre offering first. Announce that you are respectfully informing the earth deity and guardians of the home.

Say: “We respectfully inform the earth deity and guardians of this home that our family has received this place. We ask for blessing, permission, peace, safety, and smooth renovation.”
B

Four Corners Clockwise

Move clockwise to each corner. At each corner, offer incense and repeat a short request for peace and blessing.

Say: “May this corner be clean, peaceful, protected, and harmonious. May all unsuitable energy leave peacefully. May this home be safe and prosperous.”
C

Burn Paper If Doing That Tradition

Burn ritual paper only if safe and allowed. Let candles complete safely. Keep watch until everything is fully extinguished.

D

Sweep Out

After the ritual, sweep from the four corners toward the main door. Close with thanks.

Say: “Thank you for receiving our offering. May this household be peaceful, healthy, safe, and prosperous.”

After The Ritual

Once the opening sequence is complete, switch into practical site mode.
